# History loading and verification-import candidate — 2026-10-03

This candidate distinguishes a pending history query from a completed empty result or an unavailable result. Ordinary visible selection, range and destination changes preserve known resource names while invalidating points. Hide, exclusions, retention changes, Clear, lock, sleep and quit still clear names/points immediately; stale replies cannot restore them. After a required privacy clear, a generic selected-resource label avoids a blank picker without retaining its former identifying label. Existing points for the same selection remain visible during a refresh; the loading presentation replaces an empty result while work is pending.

Source ciphertext hashes remain fixture provenance; the imported disposable store and subsequent verification ingestion do not share the fixture's test key.
